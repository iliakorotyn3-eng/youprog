Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. About YouProg

What YouProg is

YouProg is a private desktop tool that runs on its owner's computer. The owner connects their own Google accounts to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

Data it accesses

With the owner's consent given on Google's sign-in screen, YouProg uses YouTube API Services to access:

  • the connected channel's analytics (views, watch time, traffic sources, audience retention, demographics in aggregate);
  • the connected channel's videos, their titles, descriptions, subtitles, thumbnails and comments;
  • public statistics of other YouTube channels and videos.

How the data is used

The data is shown to the owner and used to prepare, upload and edit videos on the owner's own channels. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone.

Where the data is kept

Access tokens and all collected data are stored only on the owner's computer. YouProg has no server of its own. Analytics data is refreshed from YouTube and is not kept longer than 30 days without being refreshed.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Disconnecting a channel inside YouProg deletes its tokens from the computer. Deleting the tool's data folder deletes everything it stored.

Third parties

YouProg uses YouTube API Services. Google's privacy policy applies to them: policies.google.com/privacy. The YouTube Terms of Service: youtube.com/t/terms.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the issues page of this site's repository on GitHub.
