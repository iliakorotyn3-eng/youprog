YouProg

YouProg is a private desktop tool. Its owner uses it on their own computer to manage their own YouTube channels: to read channel analytics, upload finished videos, set titles, descriptions, subtitles and thumbnails, and plan releases.

Who can use it

The tool is not a public service and has no sign-up. Only the owner connects their own Google accounts to it.

What it does with YouTube data

  • Reads the analytics and the video list of the connected channel to show them to the owner.
  • Uploads and edits videos on the connected channel when the owner asks for it.
  • Reads public statistics of other YouTube channels for comparison.

All data stays on the owner's computer. Details are in the privacy policy.

YouProg uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service.
